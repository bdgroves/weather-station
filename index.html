    Observed conditions come from National Weather Service stations: McChord AFB for Lakewood, Reno–Tahoe airport, the Furnace Creek visitor center in Death Valley, and the Green Spring fire-weather station for Sonora (there is no official station in town). Forecasts: NWS and Open-Meteo. Records and normals: NOAA's Applied Climate Information System, rebuilt daily. Air quality: Open-Meteo (CAMS). Everything is read live by this page every 5 minutes; a GitHub Action keeps a backup copy.
